[b][size=4]CURRENT RELEASE[/size][/b]

Mod: J.O.O.N.A.S. Joyride 1.0.2
Target game build: Moon Sweepers 1.0.5
Status: Stable 1.0.2 release

[b]Please back up your save before using any mods.[/b]



[b][size=4]FEATURES[/size][/b]

[list]
[*]Surf mode keeps you standing on top of the J.O.O.N.A.S.[/*]
[*]Tank mode lowers you into the robot so only your head sticks out.[/*]
[*]Mount and dismount nearby J.O.O.N.A.S. with the normal interact key.[/*]
[*]Switch between Surf mode and Tank mode while mounted.[/*]
[*]Cycle between first-person and third-person mounted camera views.[/*]
[*]Remembers your preferred riding mode and mounted camera between mounts and game sessions.[/*]
[/list]



[b][size=4]CONTROLS[/size][/b]

[list]
[*]E: Mount or dismount a nearby J.O.O.N.A.S.[/*]
[*]F: Switch between Surf mode and Tank mode while mounted.[/*]
[*]C: Cycle between first-person and third-person mounted camera views.[/*]
[/list]



[b][size=4]MOD MANAGER SETTINGS[/size][/b]


Diagnostics: enables verbose troubleshooting logs. Disabled by default.



[b][size=4]REQUIREMENTS[/size][/b]

Install these prerequisites before installing J.O.O.N.A.S. Joyride:

[list]
[*]BepInEx 6 IL2CPP for Moon Sweepers[/*]
[*]The Moon Sweepers BepInEx compatibility patcher required by the game[/*]
[*]MoonFramework 1.0.5.2[/*]
[*]Moon Sweepers 1.0.5[/*]
[/list]



[b][size=4]INSTALLATION[/size][/b]

[list=1]
[*]Close Moon Sweepers completely.[/*]
[*]Install and verify BepInEx and its Moon Sweepers compatibility patcher.[/*]
[*]Install MoonFramework.[/*]
[*]Extract the J.O.O.N.A.S. Joyride archive into the Moon Sweepers installation directory.[/*]
[/list]

Confirm that the plugin is located like this:

Moon Sweepers/
  BepInEx/
    plugins/
      JoonasJoyride/
        JoonasJoyride.dll



[b][size=4]NOTES[/size][/b]

This mod does not change J.O.O.N.A.S. cleaning behavior or make the robot directly drivable. It simply lets you ride along in Surf or Tank mode.

The mod was tested on Moon Sweepers 1.0.5 with the current BepInEx and MoonFramework setup. Game updates may require compatibility testing.



[b][size=4]KNOWN LIMITATIONS[/size][/b]

[list]
[*]Surf and Tank modes are riding positions, not full vehicle-driving controls.[/*]
[*]The mounted camera uses the game's vehicle-style third-person view when switched away from first person.[/*]
[*]The mod depends on the game's J.O.O.N.A.S. and player-mounting systems; Moon Sweepers updates may require compatibility testing.[/*]
[/list]



[b][size=4]UNINSTALLATION[/size][/b]

[list]
[*]Back up any saves made while using the mod.[/*]
[*]Close Moon Sweepers.[/*]
[*]Remove the BepInEx/plugins/JoonasJoyride/ folder.[/*]
[*]Leave BepInEx and MoonFramework installed if other mods use them.[/*]
[/list]
