# Settings

In your mod manager's settings for J.O.O.N.A.S. Joyride, or in `BepInEx/config/tylerun.moonsweepers.joonaspassenger.cfg` under `[J.O.O.N.A.S. Joyride]`:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Diagnostics` | off | Verbose troubleshooting lines in the BepInEx log. Turn it on only while chasing a problem. |

Your riding mode and camera view aren't settings: they're remembered for you in `BepInEx/config/JoonasJoyride.preferences`. Older versions'
settings (`[Passenger]`) are moved over automatically.
